Privacy Policy · Effective date: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Insurance Atlas handles information when you use the mobile application. Insurance Atlas is a research and intelligence application for insurance professionals and interested users.

1. Information we may process

Insurance Atlas is designed to work without requiring you to create an account. Depending on the features you use, the app may process:

  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us, such as your email address and the content of your message.
  • Local application data, such as saved research items, preferences, alert settings, and recently viewed content. This information may remain on your device and is not necessarily transmitted to us.
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure the app, such as device and operating-system information, crash details, and basic diagnostic data, where made available by the platform or hosting provider.
  • Information contained in or opened from external public sources. When you follow an external source link, that source's own privacy policy applies.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve Insurance Atlas and its research features.
  • To save your preferences and requested content on your device.
  • To respond to support, privacy, or account-deletion requests.
  • To detect, prevent, and address security, abuse, and technical problems.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information to service providers that help operate the app, where necessary and subject to appropriate safeguards, or when required by law. We do not use Insurance Atlas to collect payment-card information.

4. Data retention and deletion

Local saved items and preferences can be removed by using the relevant in-app controls, clearing the app data, or uninstalling the app. Information submitted through email is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. To request deletion of information associated with an account or request, contact us at the address below.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the information processed. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

6. Children's privacy

Insurance Atlas is intended for a professional and general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, applicable requirements, or our processing practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

8. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact: atlasinsuranceresearch@gmail.com
