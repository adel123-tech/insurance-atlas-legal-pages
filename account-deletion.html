Account Deletion · Effective date: August 25, 2026

This page explains how an Insurance Atlas user can request deletion of an account and associated personal information.

1. If you created an account

Send an email to atlasinsuranceresearch@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Use the subject line “Insurance Atlas – Account deletion request” and include the account email address or other information reasonably necessary for us to identify the account. Do not send your password.

2. What will be deleted

  • The account profile and personal information associated with the verified account, where technically and legally possible.
  • User-created content or saved cloud data associated with that account, where applicable.
  • Active account access and related authentication records, subject to the exceptions below.

3. Information that may be retained

We may retain limited information where necessary to comply with law, prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or maintain required business and security records. Retained information will be isolated and used only for the applicable purpose.

4. If you did not create an account

The current application can be used without an account. Local saved items, preferences, and cached content can be removed by using in-app controls, clearing Insurance Atlas data in your device settings, or uninstalling the application. If you contacted us by email and want that request deleted, email us at the address above.

5. Processing time and verification

We may verify the request before acting to protect account holders from unauthorized deletion. We will process a valid request within a reasonable period, subject to applicable law and the scope of the information requested.

Important: Do not include your password in an account-deletion request.

6. Contact

Account deletion requests: atlasinsuranceresearch@gmail.com
